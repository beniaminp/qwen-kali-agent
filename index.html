    The core path¶

    01 Pick the model What fits, what does not, and why Qwen3-14B is the local default. 02 Check the memory Unified-memory math, sequence length, batch and QLoRA peak. 03 Build the data Capability mix, trajectories, negative examples and quality gates. 04 Train the adapter MLX runbook, flags, checkpoints and loss reading. 05 Prove it works Benchmarks, private golden set and release gates. 06 Ship with guardrails Serving, MCP, scope enforcement, audit and kill switch.

    ✦ 2026 LoRA recipe All-layer LoRA, the MLX scale trap and one-epoch training. ◇ One model for all Qwen3-14B plus three adapters, or one mixed adapter. ⇣ Distillation Teacher logits, sequence KD and 12B → E4B/E2B compression. ↑ From zero Train a small model you can actually afford. ∑ Mathematics Attention, RoPE, GQA, MoE, Gated DeltaNet, FLOPs and cache. ▦ Architecture anatomy From config.json to tensor shapes, with diagrams. ƒ Derivations Softmax, attention backward, RMSNorm, RoPE and GDN proofs. ✓ Worked examples Every formula on a toy model, checked by a 69-value script.

    What is new¶

    29 Oct 2026 Agents, integrated and applied

    Eight ways to wire an agent into a product, the run contract and a runnable agent service, then twelve real applications end to end: support, coding in CI, data analysis, incident response, documents, research, sales, e-commerce, helpdesk, pipelines and QA.

    28 Oct 2026 New menu: AI Engineer

    What it takes to move from software engineering to AI engineering: 19 chapters from vectors and gradients to data preparation, RAG, agents, MCP, agent frameworks, agent integration and real applications, evals, serving and interviews, with on-paper examples and tested code.

    28 Oct 2026 Every formula, on paper

    129 short examples you can redo with a pencil, one after every formula in chapters 32–42, each number checked by make math-check.

    28 Oct 2026 H100 budget corrected

    Dense BF16 is ~989 TFLOP/s (1,979 is the sparse figure): Qwen3-14B on 36T tokens is ~2.24M GPU-hours, ~9.3 days and ~$5.6M, not double that.

    27 Oct 2026 Gemma 4 E2B and E4B, explained

    Per-layer embeddings, KV sharing and the double-wide MLP, read from the checkpoints — and why the E4B cache at 128K outgrows the 12B’s.

    27 Oct 2026 Worked examples, grounded

    Every formula now starts from a real situation — a next token, a weight row, an eval batch — with a corrected Wilson interval and 64 checked values.

    Authorized testing only. A model is not authorization. Use a written scope, a lab you own, and a policy layer that enforces both.

    A handbook, not a tested product. Commands were checked against upstream source/docs on 25 Oct 2026, but the MLX runs were not executed on the target Mac. Run the preflight and a smoke test before a multi-day run.
